1. **smallgroup-shows-playlist.xml** - `docs/smallgroup-shows-playlist.xml` (Small Group Shows: the latest episodes from every show on [smallgroupscoop.com/shows](https://smallgroupscoop.com/shows/), newest first)

## Publisher Feed

- **chadf-musicl-publisher.xml** - `docs/chadf-musicl-publisher.xml` (Aggregated publisher feed for all musicL playlists)

## Notes

- All playlist XML files are located in the `docs/` directory
- Some playlists have corresponding artwork files (`.webp` format)
- The publisher feed aggregates all individual playlist feeds
- Playlists are updated automatically from their source RSS feeds
- podcastL playlists are generated by their own aggregator steps in the daily update, not from this list (only musicL playlist IDs here are read by the updater)
